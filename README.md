# Tuesday 17 November 2026 — world news in one image

An original editorial poster summing up the world news of Tuesday 17 November 2026, built for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117).

## What it shows

A midnight-edition news poster: eight illustrated lead stories with large,
legible headlines, plus 36 further verified stories in a compact "Also on
Tuesday" index — 44 stories across six continents. The artwork is hand-built
from vector shapes and rendered to a crisp 1024×1024 PNG.

Lead stories: the Kerch/Crimean bridge span destroyed; Iranian drones on a US
radar in Bahrain and the US response on Qeshm; Trump's first Quad summit in
Manila; Nvidia's record $115.8bn quarter; Ebola above 11,000 cases in DR Congo
and the loss of measles-free status in the US, Mexico and Bolivia; the COP31
fossil-fuel phase-out split; Serbia's student movement sworn in; and the M6.6
Banda Sea quake.

## Program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the committed `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). No network access, no
randomness, no clock — both runs write identical bytes.
